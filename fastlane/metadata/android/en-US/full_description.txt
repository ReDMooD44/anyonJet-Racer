Canyon Jet Racer is a low-altitude arcade jet race through a narrow canyon.

Race five bots through a procedurally built canyon, chase your best lap in time
trial, and collect sector, lap and medal times. The whole track - walls,
terraces, rim and the baked shadow line - is generated from a spline in code,
so the map preview on every card is a real drawing of that track.

FEATURES

- Six maps, each with its own spline, colour mood and difficulty
- Race against five bots with a live standings panel
- Time trial with a ghost of your best lap
- 16 checkpoints, 4 sectors, lap and medal times
- Floating touch joystick, airbrake and a charged boost
- Optional gyroscope steering with adjustable sensitivity
- Everything is synthesised in code: engine, wind, effects and music.
  There are no audio files and nothing is downloaded.
- Works fully offline. No ads, no analytics, no network permission.

CONTROLS

- Left thumb: virtual stick for pitch and roll. It floats to where you press.
- Right thumb: BOOST (3 second charge) and BRAKE (airbrake while held).
- The joystick lets go and the jet levels itself, so you can look up.
- Gyroscope steering can be enabled in Settings; the left half of the screen
  then becomes the boost button.
- Press II or Android Back to pause.
